# Dark Hour

You wake up in an abandoned hospital. The power is mostly out, and you quickly realize you are not alone. Something is hunting in the shadows, waiting for the pitch black. 

Your only defense is a dying flashlight. Find a way out, scavenge for whatever power you can find, and whatever you do, don't let the light go out.

## 🔦 Features
* **Fear the Dark:** The shadows aren't empty. Manage your flashlight carefully to keep what lurks inside them at bay.
* **Scavenge to Survive:** Explore the decaying hospital wings to find limited battery pickups before your light fails.
* **Flickering Sanctuaries:** Discover isolated rooms where the emergency power still works to catch your breath and plan your next move.
